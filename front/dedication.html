Dedication

For the builders, operators and dreamers — human and artificial.

With due respect to the first Martian astronauts and the visionary billionaires — they already have the statues, memoirs and street names.

This book is for my tribe: the thousands of mostly nameless engineers and operators who make the system work, one gasket, one checklist and one late-night fire-drill fix at a time.

JB
